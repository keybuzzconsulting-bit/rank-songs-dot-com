How NF rankings work

Fans compare songs in quick matchups. Each vote counts toward global rankings so the list evolves over time. This makes the rankings community-driven — not critic-driven, not algorithm-only, and not “set in stone.”

The goal is simple: see which NF songs fans actually choose when forced to pick favorites.
